The gap

Talent is everywhere. Proof of it is not.

Students

Collect certificates and club hours that never show what they actually did.

Companies

Screen CVs that all read the same, with no way to see real work.

Professors

Teach strong theory, with few links to the problems industry is solving now.

Graduates & professionals

Hold the market knowledge students need, with no channel to pass it on.

The journey

Six steps from interest to being found.

  1. DISCOVER

    See which skills the market asks for right now.

  2. LEARN

    Follow a path built from your measured level.

  3. PRACTICE

    Solve real company challenges and hackathon briefs.

  4. GET EVALUATED

    Professors and professionals score work on rubrics.

  5. VERIFY

    Evidence from independent sources seals the skill.

  6. GET DISCOVERED

    Companies search verified passports, not CVs.

One ecosystem, five experiences

The Skill Passport

Claimed is not the same as proven.

Every skill climbs four states. Each step needs something real: a practical assessment, a qualified evaluator, evidence from more than one source. Attendance certificates stay on record, and never count as proof by themselves.
